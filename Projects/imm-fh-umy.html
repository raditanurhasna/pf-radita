Web Experience

IMM FH UMY

The official website design for a student organization, built as an information hub for law students.

Introduction

A clear home on the web for a student organization

Role

UI/UX Designer

Project

Client project, team of 3

Duration

1 month

Tools

Figma, WordPress

This project started when IMM UMY asked me to design the look of their website, so students of the Faculty of Law would have one official place to learn about the organization.

Overview

The IMM FH UMY website delivers the organization's profile, news, management structure, and contact information to students of the Faculty of Law, Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta.

Request

The organization needed an official website that presents its information in a structured, accessible way and works as a central digital hub.

Users

Law students at Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta who want to follow organizational updates and find information about the organization.

Scope

Page and content mapping, wireframes, high-fidelity UI in Figma, a Figma prototype, and the WordPress implementation of the design.

The brief

What the website had to do

The request came directly from the organization, so the starting point was clear: students needed to find four kinds of information quickly and without confusion.

1

Organization profile

Who the organization is and what it stands for.

2

News and updates

A place to follow what the organization is doing.

3

Management structure

Who is in the management team.

4

Contact information

A clear way for students to reach the organization.

Rising action

Mapping the pages before designing them

What I created wireframes for every page of the website.

Why The layout, content hierarchy, and navigation had to be clear before any visual styling.

How I mapped each page so the profile, news, management structure, and contact details would be presented clearly and be easy to scan for student visitors.

The climax

From wireframe to final design

What A clean, professional interface that matches the organization's identity.

Why The website represents the organization, so it had to look credible and still be easy to read.

How I designed the high-fidelity screens in Figma. Four design decisions shaped them:

1

Red, gray, and black from the logo

The colors follow the IMM logo and were also a request from the organization, so the website feels like it belongs to them.

2

A clean, professional look

The interface stays simple and aligned with the identity of the Faculty of Law.

3

Readability first

The layout prioritizes readable text so students can take in news and information without effort.

4

Easy navigation

Students can quickly find the profile, news, structure, and contact details from one central hub.

Resolution

What came out of it

Result

A structured website design that gives law students one place to find the organization's profile, news, management structure, and contact information, designed in Figma and built in WordPress by our team of three.

Feedback

The organization found the design easy to understand, and the colors matched what they asked for.

Lesson learned

This project gave me hands-on experience designing an organizational website from wireframe to interface in Figma. Implementing it in WordPress taught me how designs are applied in a real website, and the importance of balancing visual design with functional requirements, so the result looks professional and still works as an information hub for students.
